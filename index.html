How it works:

  1. A customer deposits new money (e.g., from the Central Bank).
  2. The bank keeps a fraction as Reserves.
  3. The bank lends out the rest (Excess Reserves).
  4. The loan is spent and deposited into another bank.
  5. The cycle repeats, multiplying the initial money.
